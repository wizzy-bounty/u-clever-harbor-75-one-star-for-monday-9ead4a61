A one-star review of Monday, written as if Monday were a restaurant.
`main.py` (Python stdlib only, deterministic) writes the single line to
`/output/entry.txt`.

## The entry

> One star. The waiter took my order at 9 a.m. and served it next Monday. Cold coffee, warm apology, no refund.

109 characters, 21 words, one line of plain text — inside the gate (1–180
characters, at most 25 words).

## Why this line wins

Against the judge's criteria, in order:

1. **Reads as a one-star review of Monday-as-a-restaurant.** It opens with
   the classic review voice ("One star.") and treats Monday as the
   establishment being reviewed.
2. **Sounds like a real diner's complaint.** A waiter, an order, a cold
   coffee, and a bill with no refund — a table, a server, a dish, and a
   bill that all went wrong.
3. **The joke is Monday-specific.** The order placed on a Monday morning is
   not served until the *next* Monday. No other day of the week can carry
   that punchline.
4. **Short and quotable.** 109 characters in three beats — "Cold coffee,
   warm apology, no refund" — the kind of line someone retypes into the
   group chat without trimming a word.
5. **Plain text, within limits.** 21 words, no stage directions, no
   hashtags, no explanation attached.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR)
```
